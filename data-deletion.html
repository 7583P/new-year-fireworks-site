Data Deletion Request

App: New Year Fireworks Playground

Developer: Matias Games

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of personal data associated with New Year Fireworks Playground, contact:

matiasgamesdev.support@gmail.com

Use the subject line: New Year Fireworks Playground - Data Deletion Request.

Describe the data you want deleted. We may request limited additional information to identify relevant records or verify the request. Do not send passwords or payment card information.

What Data Can Be Deleted?

Depending on the data available and the services involved, deletion requests may cover personal information or identifiers associated with the app.

Game progress, rankings, preferences and other locally stored data may be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not necessarily remove information retained by third-party providers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses services that may process data independently, including Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and Unity services.

We will review requests relating to data under our control and, where applicable, direct users to the relevant service provider's privacy or data deletion procedures.

Data Retention

Some information may need to be retained for legal, financial, security, fraud prevention or transaction verification purposes.

Applicable retention periods depend on the type of data and the service provider. We will explain any applicable retention requirement when responding to a request.
