Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to New Year Fireworks Playground, an Android game published by Matias Games Dev. It explains how information is processed through the Game and its integrated services, including Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about how information is accessed, collected, used, stored and shared when you interact with our Game.

This policy also explains your privacy choices, how to request assistance with your information, and how third-party service providers may process data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the Game.

1.1 Gameplay Information

The Game may process gameplay-related information, including:

  • Game scores and achievements.
  • Gameplay progress and completed objectives.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Progress saved locally on the device.
  • Leaderboard information where the corresponding feature is enabled.
  • Saved game data where cloud saving is enabled.

This information is used to provide gameplay features and maintain the player's experience.

1.2 Google Play Games Information

When Google Play Games Services is used, Google may provide the Game with information associated with the authenticated player.

Depending on the permissions and functionality enabled, this may include:

  • Google Play Games player identifier.
  • Player display name and avatar.
  • Achievement information.
  • Scores and leaderboard rankings.
  • Saved game information when the Saved Games feature is enabled.

This information is used only to provide supported Google Play Games functionality.

1.3 Advertising and Technical Information

The Google Mobile Ads SDK used for AdMob may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address.
  • Approximate geographical location inferred from the IP address.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • App usage and interaction information.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Diagnostic information, including performance and crash-related data.

These data categories may vary according to operating system settings, advertising configuration, consent choices and applicable law.

2. Google Play Games Services

New Year Fireworks Playground integrates with Google Play Games Services to support applicable player and gameplay functionality.

Depending on the enabled features, Google Play Games Services may be used for:

  • Player authentication.
  • Displaying the player's gaming profile.
  • Achievements.
  • Leaderboards and scores.
  • Saving or restoring game progress.

When authentication occurs, the Game may receive a player identifier and profile information necessary to provide the selected Google Play Games features.

The Game uses information obtained through Google Play Games APIs for player-facing gameplay features and related functionality.

We do not use Google Play Games player data to create advertising profiles, and we do not sell Google Play Games player information.

We do not share Google Play Games player data with advertising providers for personalized advertising.

Google may process Google Play Games information in accordance with its own privacy policies.

For additional details, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Advertisements may include banner ads, rewarded video ads, or other supported formats, depending on the features available in the Game.

3.1 Advertising Data

Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, usage information, advertising interactions and diagnostic data.

3.2 Purposes of Advertising Data Processing

  • Delivering advertisements.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Detecting advertising fraud and abuse.
  • Maintaining advertising service security.
  • Improving advertising services.
  • Personalizing advertisements where permitted and configured, subject to applicable consent requirements.

3.3 Personalized Advertising and Consent

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent must be obtained before the use of personal information for personalized advertising.

Advertising choices may also be available through Google Account settings, Android privacy settings, and any consent controls provided within the Game.

For more information, visit: Google's Advertising Privacy Information .

4. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with the Game is used for the following purposes, where applicable:

  • Providing game functionality.
  • Maintaining gameplay progress.
  • Authenticating players with Google Play Games Services.
  • Providing achievements and leaderboards.
  • Supporting saved game functionality.
  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Detecting fraud and unauthorized activity.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving stability.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used for the purposes described in this policy and applicable user-facing features.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The Game integrates with third-party technology providers.

5.1 Google Play Games Services

Google may process player identifiers, profile information, gameplay achievements, scores and saved game information when the corresponding functionality is enabled.

5.2 Google AdMob

Google and its advertising technology partners may process advertising-related information to provide, measure, secure and improve advertising services.

5.3 Google Play Billing

If in-app purchases are offered, payment transactions are processed through Google Play.

We do not directly collect or store payment card numbers.

Purchase-related information necessary for delivering purchased features may be processed by the Game and Google Play.

We do not sell Google Play Games player information to data brokers or advertisers.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Game preferences and gameplay progress may be stored locally on the player's device.

Where Google Play Games cloud functionality is enabled, relevant player information and saved game data may be stored or processed through Google's services.

Local information may remain on the device until it is cleared, replaced or removed through applicable device or application controls.

Data stored or processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention policies and user controls.

We retain information under our direct control only for as long as reasonably necessary for its disclosed purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting player information.

Google Play Games Services and Google advertising services provide security measures for information processed through their systems.

We seek to limit access to player information to the functionality necessary to operate the Game.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

You may also have choices regarding advertising personalization and Google Play Games activity.

8.1 Google Account Controls

You can review available Google Account privacy settings at:

https://myaccount.google.com/

8.2 Google Play Games Data

Google provides controls for managing Google Play Games profile information and associated game data.

Depending on the service and available controls, players may be able to delete their Google Play Games data for an individual game.

8.3 Advertising Choices

You can manage certain advertising privacy preferences through your Google Account and Android settings.

Visit: Google My Ad Center .

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to request deletion of information controlled by the Game's developer, please contact:

Email: matiasgamesdev.support@gmail.com

Subject: New Year Fireworks Playground - Data Deletion Request

Please describe the request and provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant game data.

Do not send your Google Account password or other sensitive credentials.

We will review requests concerning data under our control and respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Information managed directly by Google may need to be deleted through the relevant Google Account or Google Play Games controls.

Uninstalling the Game may remove certain locally stored information, but it does not necessarily delete information retained through Google services.

10. Children's Privacy

We recognize that children's privacy requires special protection.

The Game's intended audience, advertising configuration and available features must comply with applicable child privacy requirements and Google Play policies.

Where applicable, the Game must apply age-appropriate advertising restrictions, consent mechanisms and data-processing safeguards.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning their children's information.

11. International Data Processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries outside the country where you live.

Such processing is subject to applicable legal requirements and the privacy protections implemented by the relevant service providers.

12. In-App Purchases

If the Game offers optional purchases, transactions are handled through Google Play Billing.

Google Play may process purchase information according to its applicable policies.

The developer does not directly receive complete payment card details.

13. Permissions

The Game may request Android or Google service permissions necessary for enabled functionality.

Access to Google Play Games data is limited to the permissions requested and authorized for the supported gameplay features.

The Game does not require users to provide their home address or telephone number to play.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's functionality, data-processing practices, third-party integrations or legal obligations change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing materially different processing of personal information.
