Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers New Year Fireworks Playground (Android package com.matias.newyearfireworkplayground), published by Matias Games Dev. It explains local gameplay information, purchases, advertising, diagnostics, third-party services and privacy choices.

The data processed depends on which functions are enabled, the device, applicable laws, consent choices and the services a player uses. Offline gameplay is available without a Google Play Games login.

1. Information We Collect and Process

1.1 Gameplay Information

The Game stores information such as progress, scores, offline ranked placements, MMR, gameplay records, a persistent offline bot leaderboard, virtual currency balances, acquired items, purchases and entitlements, settings, and recovery records on the device. This is used for gameplay, persistence, restoration and protection against repeated rewards. Locally stored records are not necessarily transmitted to us.

1.2 Google Play Games Information

Google Play Games authentication, online human leaderboards and cloud saves are disabled in the current 1.1.3 release while the integration is being completed. If activated in a later update, the service may process the authenticated player identifier, display name, avatar, achievements, scores and cloud-saved progress. We will update this policy and the relevant disclosures as appropriate before enabling these features. The existing offline bot leaderboard does not require Google Play Games.

1.3 Advertising and Technical Information

Google AdMob and related advertising technology may collect or process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad impressions, diagnostics and technical information to serve and measure ads, maintain security and prevent invalid traffic. Processing depends on the SDK configuration, consent and applicable regulations.

1.4 Purchases and Diagnostic Information

The Game integrates Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP 5.4.1) with Google Play Billing for in-app products and VIP subscriptions. The purchasing SDK and providers may process purchase history, transaction and order information, purchase-related identifiers, approximate location and technical diagnostics. Some SDK disclosures also describe user identifiers and email addresses; their applicability depends on the store and actual information exposed by the integration. The current offline game does not ask players to create a developer account or enter their email address for gameplay.

Unity's diagnostic components may process crash logs, diagnostics, other app performance data and device identifiers for stability and troubleshooting. These services can transmit data even when gameplay progress is otherwise stored locally.

2. Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games features are not currently enabled for sign-in, online leaderboards or Saved Games. A future release may provide player authentication, profile display, achievements, leaderboards and cloud save/restore, after the integration is enabled and validated. We will not treat future functionality as currently active. Google may process information under its own Privacy Policy. Google Play Games player data is not sold or provided to advertising providers by us for personalized advertising.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob for banner and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may grant in-game benefits after eligible completion; choosing to view one is optional. AdMob and advertising partners may collect and share certain data to serve advertisements, analyze performance, detect fraud and comply with requirements.

3.1 Advertising Data

Such data may include IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, usage data, ad impressions and interactions, and app diagnostics.

3.2 Purposes

Purposes include providing advertisements, analytics and measurement, service operation, fraud prevention, safety and security, and personalization where configured and legally permitted.

3.3 Consent and Advertising Choices

Where legally required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may present consent and privacy choices. Options vary by region and configuration. Users can also review Google My Ad Center and Android ad privacy settings. Non-personalized advertising may still involve data processing needed for delivery, security or measurement. See Google advertising privacy information.

4. How We Use Information

Information is processed, as applicable, to operate gameplay, preserve local progress, provide virtual goods and VIP entitlements, restore purchases, prevent repeated rewards and fraud, show and measure advertisements, operate consent controls, diagnose failures and comply with legal requirements. Google Play Games features, if introduced later, would process relevant player information for those enabled features.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

5.1 Google Play Games Services

Currently disabled for authentication and cloud features. If enabled in the future, Google may process player profile data, achievements, scores and saved games under Google's terms and privacy policies.

5.2 Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform

Google and its advertising partners may process and share advertising-related data, including device or other identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics, to provide and measure ads, manage consent, maintain security and prevent invalid traffic.

5.3 Google Play Billing

Payments are processed by Google Play. The Game can query product information, purchase status and entitlements to grant or restore virtual goods and VIP. We do not directly receive or store complete credit-card or bank-account numbers. Google applies its own privacy and retention policies to payment processing.

5.4 Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP 5.4.1)

The Game uses Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4.1 as the integration layer for purchases through Google Play Billing. Unity IAP and its associated services may process purchase history and details needed to initialize the store, retrieve purchases, grant or restore entitlements, acknowledge transactions, support fraud prevention, maintain app functionality and provide purchasing analytics or diagnostics.

Depending on the platform, the SDK and the services it uses may process approximate location, purchase history, user or device identifiers, technical performance and crash information and, in some configurations, account-associated email information. The inclusion of a type in the SDK's general disclosure does not mean that the Game's own interface asks for it or that every data type is transmitted in every purchase. The exact information depends on the installed SDK, native store responses and enabled services.

Purchase confirmation status and local entitlement records may be retained on the device so that coins and VIP are not delivered repeatedly. Unity and Google may separately retain data under their own policies. For additional details, see Unity IAP Google Play data safety information, Unity IAP privacy overview, Unity Privacy Policy, and Google Privacy Policy.

5.5 Unity Diagnostics

Unity's engine diagnostics may process crash logs, app diagnostics, other performance information and device identifiers to help diagnose errors and operate the Game reliably. These records are distinct from the offline competitive leaderboard stored on the player's device.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay progress, bot profiles, balances, settings and recovery records may be stored on the device until cleared, overwritten by authorized game functions or removed through device/app controls. Copies may exist in device-level backups. Purchase providers, advertising providers and diagnostics services retain and delete information under their respective policies and legal obligations. We retain any information under our direct control only for as long as needed for its disclosed purpose or applicable legal requirements. We do not claim a universal automatic deletion period for third-party records.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services involved. Official Google and Unity services describe encryption in transit for their supported data flows. No system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Offline local game files and third-party platform data may be subject to different safeguards.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of data we control. Manage Google account settings at Google Account, advertising preferences at My Ad Center, and any consent options made available in the Game. Google Play Games account-data controls will become relevant if the feature is activated in a future version.

9. Data Deletion Requests

For requests concerning information under the developer's control, contact:

Email: matiasgamesdev.support@gmail.com

Subject: New Year Fireworks Playground - Data Deletion Request

Public instructions: Data Deletion Request

Describe the data concerned, without sharing passwords, full card numbers or other sensitive credentials. We may ask for limited details needed to locate records and verify a request. We will respond in accordance with applicable law. Deleting local app data or uninstalling does not necessarily delete data retained by Google, Unity or advertising providers; requests concerning those records may require their own privacy tools.

10. Children's Privacy

We recognize that children's information requires special protections. Age-related advertising, consent and privacy safeguards are applied where required by law and Google Play policies. Parents and guardians may contact us through the email above about privacy inquiries. The applicable age audience and advertising configuration are those declared in Google Play Console.

11. International Data Processing

Google, Unity and their service providers may process information outside the country where you live, subject to applicable protections and privacy requirements.

12. In-App Purchases

Optional virtual goods and VIP subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing integrated with Unity IAP. The Game may store entitlement and transaction-status information locally for purchase verification, recovery and prevention of duplicated delivery. Payment card details are handled by the store, not entered into the Game. Billing support and refunds are governed by the applicable Google Play purchase terms.

13. Permissions

The Game and its integrated SDKs use Android permissions required for supported functions such as networking, billing and advertising, subject to the final app manifest and platform rules. The Game does not require players to enter their home address or telephone number for normal offline gameplay.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy when enabled features, SDKs, data practices or laws change. Updates will appear here with a new date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent before materially different processing.
